What is Zakat?
Zakat is 2.5% of your qualifying wealth, payable annually if you've held the nisab threshold for one lunar year (haul). Nisab is approximately 85g of gold or 595g of silver in current market value. In Brunei, Zakat is administered by MUIB (Majlis Ugama Islam Brunei).

🕌 How to Pay Your Zakat in Brunei
• MUIB (Majlis Ugama Islam Brunei) administers Zakat collection and distribution in Brunei.
• Pay at any MUIB office, major mosques, or via BIBD Zakat online payment.
• Keep a record — Zakat is distributed to 8 categories (asnaf) per Quran 9:60.
• Reminder: Zakat is calculated on your haul date (the Islamic date you first exceeded nisab) each lunar year.
⚠ Not a fatwa. This calculator uses common Shafi'i methodology. Consult a mufti at MUIB for specific rulings — especially on investments, jointly owned assets, or complex structures.
