● About

Hi, I'm Qawiy.

I'm an engineer by training, though these days most of my time goes into building things that make money feel less intimidating. I started investing in 2017 — gold first — and co-founded Qafahaus, a women's clothing business, back in 2016, still running strong ten years later. In 2021 I started sharing tips on productivity and personal finance on YouTube, and now I'm building Qapital: a free app for anyone who's ever thought finance was too hard to bother with.
