My Journey

From offshore wells to building ideas.

My professional journey began in mechanical engineering and developed into more than a decade in Brunei’s oil and gas industry. As an offshore well engineer, I have supported complex well operations, managed people and learned the importance of preparation, leadership and responsibility.

Beyond engineering, I am a co-founder of Qafa Haus, a financial education content creator, and the builder of Qapital Brunei. I am interested in how practical knowledge, technology and entrepreneurship can help people make better decisions.

This website documents what I am learning, creating and contributing — including the successes, challenges and lessons along the way.
